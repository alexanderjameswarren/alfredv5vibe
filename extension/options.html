Alfred's Clipboard

Two settings, once per machine. They are stored only on this computer.

How to clip: open a normal web page and click the toolbar icon, or press Ctrl+Shift+Y. A green tick means it saved. A red ! means it failed — click the icon to read why.

Chrome will flash a “Alfred's Clipboard is debugging this browser” banner while it takes the screenshot. That is expected: it is the only way to photograph a whole page rather than just the visible part.

Chrome pages, other extensions, the Web Store and PDFs cannot be clipped — Chrome blocks extensions from reading them.
